# Bunkialo v1.5

Assignments, campus contacts, home-screen widgets, and notifications get a substantial update.

## Assignments
- Faster loading: assignment details appear before submission options finish loading, with cached data and shared requests.
- A refreshed page themed to your course color, with formatted instructions, resource files, submitted files, and secondary details on demand.
- Course-name navigation without repeated titles or course codes. Compact relative dates toggle to exact dates with a tap.
- Assignment files and your submission are clearly separated, with status beside its details. Add files opens the picker directly for file assignments; Submit appears after selection. Picking files never submits automatically.
- Submission controls stay near the bottom, with LMS ↗ alongside them. File limits appear when adding a submission; uploading and submitting remain explicit actions.
- Upload progress stays within 0–100%. LMS links open the assignment page rather than the edit route.
- Android receives images and PDFs from the native share menu. Pick an assignment ordered by its nearest deadline, upload a draft, then submit when ready.

## Attachments
- PDF first-page and image previews in assignments, course resources, folders, and announcement posts.
- Built-in PDF page scrolling and zoom, with Open outside.
- Files persist and are reused after restarts; the new Android PDF build saves explicit downloads to system Downloads and opens those saved files. Deleted files download again when needed.
- Older Android builds retain external PDF viewing and persistent app-local downloads through compatible OTA updates.

## Timetable
- Regular classes show only their name and time; session-type badges are reserved for labs.
- Faculty-recorded start and end times snap to the nearest hour or half-hour before recurring-slot inference, so small timing shifts merge into one slot with combined attendance evidence. Early AM/PM typos merge only when a matching daytime slot has stronger evidence for that course and weekday. Original attendance records and manual timetable entries remain intact.

## Home-screen widgets
- Expo SDK 58 powers minimal timetable and mess-menu widgets, with course and meal colors that match the app.
- Timetable widgets show current and upcoming classes. Mess widgets show the next meal with a simple dotted list and controls for browsing meals.

## Wardens and faculty
- Hostel wardens replace the previous top-faculty section and use existing faculty profiles.
- A clear hostel selector groups hostels that share wardens. Manimala | MJ Apartment is the default; your choice persists across restarts and app updates.
- Hostel choices have distinct rows, separators, and a highlighted selection, with room for long hostel groups.
- Recent faculty searches appear in a compact dropdown from the focused search bar, without taking permanent space.
- Faculty photos download in the background, starting with the default wardens, and persist locally for later visits.
- The official IIIT Kottayam faculty scrape now includes wardens. A monthly workflow refreshes and commits the roster.

## Calendar and notifications
- Private, confidential, and busy-only club entries are hidden immediately, including previously cached entries.
- Attendance notices use subject names instead of course codes, with less repeated text and distinct icons for each notification type.
- Refined notification filters, unread counts, persistent read status, offline acknowledgement retries, and safer bulk actions that preserve newly arriving notices.
- Read cards soften only their own content; expanding restores clarity. Older Android versions use dimming where blur is unavailable.
- Independent Android attendance polling uses the 15-minute background minimum, with retry receipts and catch-up when the app resumes. A battery-settings shortcut helps manage device restrictions; Android still controls actual background execution.
- Calendar reminders respect explicit reminder/deadline data, with a 30-minute default, and exclude private, hidden, and tentative events.

## Compatibility and release controls
- Android is the native target; web/PWA remains the iPhone and iPad experience.
- Build, release, and OTA workflows run only when manually triggered. The monthly roster refresh is the scheduled exception.
- Compatible app improvements are also published to existing SDK 54 and SDK 58 runtimes. Widgets and native share registration require a matching SDK 58 installation.
- Server-side PWA background notification scheduling is implemented and tested; activating it on the hosted web app still requires deployment credentials.
